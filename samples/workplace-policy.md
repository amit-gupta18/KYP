# Northwind Workplace Policy

This sample policy is original text you can upload to try Policy QA. It is not legal advice.

## Working hours

The standard week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time zone. People may start between 08:00 and 10:00 if their manager agrees.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 18 paid annual leave days each calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and can be used after the first 30 days of employment. A maximum of 5 unused days carries into the next year and must be taken by 31 March. Requests of more than 10 consecutive working days need director approval.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days each calendar year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive working days. Unused sick days do not carry over and are not paid out.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Leave can start up to 2 weeks before the expected arrival date. The manager and People team must be told at least 8 weeks in advance when the date is known.

## Remote work

Employees may work from home up to 3 days each week. The team must keep one shared in-office day, chosen by the manager. Working from another country for more than 10 working days in a quarter requires People team approval because of tax and security rules.

## Code of conduct

Colleagues, customers, and candidates are treated with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are not allowed. Gifts from suppliers above USD 50 must be declined or reported to the manager. Company systems are not used for outside business.

## Confidential information

Customer data, pricing, and unreleased product plans are confidential. They are not sent to personal email or stored in personal cloud drives. A suspected leak is reported to security@northwind.example the same day it is noticed.

## Data retention

Customer support tickets are kept for 24 months and then deleted. Employee files are kept for 6 years after the person leaves. Expense receipts are kept for 7 years. People may ask People Operations for a copy of their employee file.

## Grievances

A workplace concern can be raised with the manager, or directly with People Operations if the manager is involved. People Operations replies within 5 working days and aims to close the review within 20 working days. Retaliation for raising a concern in good faith is a conduct breach.

## Travel and expenses

Trips need manager approval before booking. Economy airfare is the standard. Hotel stays are capped at USD 220 per night before tax, or USD 320 in New York, London, and Singapore. Meals on overnight trips are capped at USD 70 per day. Receipts are required for any expense above USD 25. Claims are submitted within 30 days of the trip.
